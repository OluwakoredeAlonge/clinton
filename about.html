About the artist

A life spent mixing the right red.

Clinton Vane paints full time from a converted foundry studio. The work spans landscape, portraiture, seascape, still life and pure abstraction, in oil, acrylic, watercolour, gouache, ink and pastel, often at a scale that asks the viewer to step back, then step in.

Every year or two the studio closes its doors, and the year's work leaves for a gallery. Each exhibition is built as a journey: rooms with their own mood, a curator's hand, and a catalogue to take home.

The process

Sketch

Months of drawings, colour notes and walks before a single canvas is stretched.

Build

Thin glazes over a toned ground, each layer left to settle before the next.

Scrape

Knives, rags and patience: removing what the painting doesn't need.

Hang

The work isn't finished until it is on a wall, in the right light.
